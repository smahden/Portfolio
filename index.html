01. About Me

Hello! I'm Mahden, a software engineer who enjoys turning ideas into working products. My path into engineering started with curiosity about how the apps I used every day actually worked — and quickly turned into building my own.

Today I work across the stack: designing REST APIs and data models on the back end, and building fast, accessible interfaces on the front end. I'm comfortable owning a feature end-to-end — from requirements and design through implementation, testing, code review, and deployment.
